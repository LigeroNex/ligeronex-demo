TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Trust is part of
the product.

Enterprise buyers ask these questions first, so the answers are built into how the products work rather than added as policy afterwards.

The employee chooses

People opt in per activity and pick their own topics. A recommendation is never a connection — both sides decide, and either can decline.

Explainable by design

Every match shows the stored factors behind it in plain language. If a reason cannot be shown, the recommendation is not made.

Your data stays yours

Each product runs in its own environment with its own tenant boundary. Nothing moves between products, and nothing between customers.

Designed against real frameworks

ICO guidance on monitoring workers, the EU AI Act and NIST's AI Risk Management Framework shaped the design from day one.
